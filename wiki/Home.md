# QuietVote

> **Draft – unaudited software, not for real elections yet.** QuietVote is a plan and an early build. Do not use it for a real vote. See [[Roadmap and Status]].

**QuietVote lets a group vote in secret and still check the result.** It is for clubs, co-ops, unions, open-source groups and online communities. Members vote in secret. Anyone can check that the final count is right. You do not have to trust the people who run it.

## Important

- QuietVote does **not** protect you if someone watches you vote, if your phone is hacked, or if someone forces you to vote a certain way. In version 1, others can see **that** you voted, but not **how**. Read [[Privacy and Guarantees]] before you rely on it.
- The detailed rules are in the specifications under `docs/` (see [[Specification Index]]). These wiki pages are simple summaries.
