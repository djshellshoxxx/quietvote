# QuietVote

> **Draft – unaudited software.** QuietVote is at the specification and early-build stage. It is **not** ready for real elections, and nothing here is an approved guarantee. See [[Roadmap and Status]].

**QuietVote is a private, verifiable decision-making tool for voluntary groups** such as cooperatives, member associations, unions, open-source foundations and online collectives. Members vote on a secret ballot, and anyone can check that the published result is correct, without having to trust the operator.
