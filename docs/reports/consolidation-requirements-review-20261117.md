## Genuine contract choices still needed

The governance RFC names precise unresolved semantics, not a blanket prohibition on implementation. They include authenticated elapsed-time bounds, dynamic group snapshot timing, the informal template's 48-hour voting versus 72-hour global minimum and 24-hour review versus 168-hour class minimum, recovery-amendment accounting, late additions/denominator effects, confidential dispute evidence, timeout behavior and arbiter randomness/fallback (`docs/rfcs/governance-v1-contract.md:180–224,244–249`). Isolated parsers, signed history, CLI and negative cases can proceed; production routes cannot manufacture trusted elapsed-time or ratification evidence.

The initial genesis signature and domain choices in the RFC are already concretized for the isolated core in `docs/GOVERNANCE-CORE.md`. Do not present them as wholly unimplemented or restart their design without a specific defect.
