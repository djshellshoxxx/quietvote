# QV-10 — User Experience

**Module ID:** `experience-accessibility`  
**Status:** Framework  
**Objective:** Make rules, privacy limits, participation, and verification understandable to members and administrators.

## Primary flows to design

- Join an organization and learn its privacy model
- Receive and verify eligibility
- Review a proposal and ballot rules
- Submit a ballot and receive a non-revealing confirmation
- Verify that a ballot was accepted, if the selected protocol supports it
- View results and audit evidence
- Challenge or report a process problem
- Create, review, open, close, and archive a decision
- Recover account or credential access

## UX requirements

- Show the exact rules and deadlines before ballot submission.
- Distinguish “private from other members” from “hidden from organizers/operators.”
- Avoid confirmation screens that reveal a user's selected choice in shared environments.
- Explain failed, pending, duplicate, and accepted submissions.
- Make changes to ballot rules and eligibility visible and attributable.
- Provide clear recovery and support paths.
- Document behavior when a user loses connectivity or a service is unavailable.

## Research and design artifacts

- Target-user interviews and findings
- Task flows and wireframes
- Privacy comprehension test
- Usability test plan and measured tasks
- Content glossary for governance and cryptography terms
- Error-state and dispute-state designs

## Open questions

- What is the member's minimum technical skill?
- Which device and connectivity conditions are common?
- How much cryptographic detail belongs in the main flow versus an audit view?
- Should users choose real-name or pseudonymous display per group or per decision?
