## Standing responses

1. Any Critical-band risk that trips its trigger is escalated to the project owner within one week and entered in the decision log.
2. Residual risks of class "known limitation" (client delivery, compromised devices, coercion, Phase 1 participation visibility) are not closed by mitigation; they are **disclosed** in docs and UI (ADR-008, ADR-010).
3. Risks are re-scored at M0, M1, M3, M5, M6, M8.
