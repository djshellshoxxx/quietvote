## Notes for reviewers

1. **Count:** 36 tooltip keys. 24 are guarantee (G) rows and need protocol-owner approval before release. The key list is a proposal, not an approved catalog.
2. **Length:** every English string above is at most 140 characters. Check with the readability script (QV-10 REQ-025) before review. The strings were written, not measured.
3. **Overlap with existing copy:** several rows repeat existing keys in `en.ts`. Where the tooltip repeats a guarantee, the existing key text is authoritative. Do not reword a G row without approval.
4. **Not a substitute for visible copy:** rows for `qv.tip.test.publicChoices`, `qv.tip.privacy.coercion` and `qv.tip.org.freeze` must also be visible on the page, not only in a tooltip.
5. **Trigger rules:** tooltips open on focus and on hover; they stay open until dismissed (Escape or blur); they are reachable by keyboard and touch (tap "?"). Content on hover or focus must be dismissible, hoverable and persistent (WCAG 1.4.13 `[UNVERIFIED: not retrieved in this pass]`).
6. **Sources:** SNIPPET = seen in a search excerpt; UNVERIFIED = not checked. Full source list is in `docs/research/ui-security-ux.md` §6.
